Hector Uploader

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Hector Uploader (the "App"), a personal tool operated by Double0skys, uses and why. In short: the App only uploads the owner's own videos to the owner's own TikTok account, and it does not collect anyone else's data.

1. Information the App uses

When the owner signs in with TikTok, TikTok gives the App only what the owner approves:

  • Access tokens that let the App post to the owner's TikTok account.
  • Basic account information (such as the TikTok account ID, display name and avatar) to show which account is connected.
  • The videos and captions the owner chooses to upload.

2. Information the App does not collect

  • No data about other TikTok users, followers, viewers or commenters.
  • No contacts, messages, location, or device identifiers.
  • No tracking cookies, advertising or analytics tools. This website doesn't use cookies either.

3. How the information is used

Only to upload the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok account and to show which account is connected. It is never used for advertising or profiling.

4. Sharing

The information is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. Videos and captions are sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official API, to be posted. TikTok handles them under its own Privacy Policy.

5. Storage and security

Access tokens are kept privately on the owner's own device and are not sent to any other server. Video files stay on the owner's device apart from the copy uploaded to TikTok.

6. Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept only while the App is connected. To disconnect, remove the App in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy › Security › Manage app permissions; its tokens then stop working, and the stored copies are deleted. Videos already posted can be deleted in TikTok.

7. Children

The App is not meant for children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in their country).

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on the App's GitHub page.
